Privacy Policy

X7F3A9 is a private personal analytics utility operated for the sole use of its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not provide public user accounts or registration.

Data accessed

When authorized through Google OAuth, the utility may access data from the owner's connected Google account that is necessary for analytics and reporting. Depending on the enabled Google API scopes, this may include account-authorized analytics, channel or content metadata, comments and related reporting data.

Purpose of use

Google user data is used only to generate private analytics and reports for the account owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling third parties, or unrelated purposes.

Storage

Authorized data may be stored in private systems controlled by the owner for historical analysis and reporting. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored as secrets and are not intentionally exposed in public source code or public reports.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold. It is not shared with third parties except where technically necessary to operate the owner's private infrastructure or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data may be retained for historical analytics. The owner may delete stored data and revoke the application's Google account access at any time through the Google Account permissions interface.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the utility's data access or processing changes materially.
