Privacy Policy

X7F3A9 is a personal analytics utility operated solely for the account owner's own use. It is not a public service and does not provide public registration or third-party user accounts.

Information accessed through Google APIs

When the owner authorizes X7F3A9 through Google OAuth, the application may access only the Google account data permitted by the granted scopes. Depending on the enabled APIs, this can include:

  • analytics and reporting metrics associated with the owner's account;
  • content, channel, or account metadata needed to identify and organize reports;
  • comments, replies, and related engagement data where the owner has authorized access;
  • reporting dimensions and historical metrics made available by the applicable Google APIs.

How the information is used

Google user data is used only to retrieve, organize, calculate, and display private analytics and reports for the account owner. It is not used for advertising, third-party profiling, credit decisions, surveillance, or any unrelated purpose.

Data collection

X7F3A9 does not collect data from unrelated users. The application processes only data that the owner explicitly authorizes through Google OAuth and data derived from those authorized analytics and reporting responses.

Storage and security

Authorized data and derived reports may be stored in private systems controlled by the owner for historical comparison and analysis. OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored as protected secrets and are not intentionally included in public source code, public pages, or public reports. Access to stored analytics is restricted to the owner and the owner's private infrastructure.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers only when technically necessary to operate the owner's private analytics workflow, and may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention

Analytics data and derived reports may be retained for historical analysis. The owner determines the retention period and may delete stored reports or source data at any time.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke X7F3A9's Google account access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access prevents future API access. Stored analytics and derived reports can also be deleted from the owner's systems independently of OAuth revocation.

Google API Services User Data Policy

X7F3A9's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data access, processing, or storage practices materially change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
